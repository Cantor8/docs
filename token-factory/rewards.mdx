Activity rewards are the public Canton featured-app program. Cantor8 handles rewards for your instrument. Cantor8 pays you and your partner venues on the agreed schedule.

This is separate from minting the instrument. You can launch and operate an instrument with rewards turned off. Token App does not post rewards. You run Token App for admin operations only: mint, burn, pause, and freeze.

<Note>
On a private synchronizer the instrument still issues and transfers. Rewards pay out when the featured-app right lives on a network that runs this program. A private synchronizer does not, by itself, pay them.
</Note>

## Who is rewarded

| Activity                                        | Rewarded |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Activity at a partner venue                     | Yes      |
| Activity you submit from your own participant   | No       |

A venue is a participant that submits transactions of your instrument. You tell Cantor8 the share each venue gets. You receive the rest.

## What Cantor8 does

- Cantor8 posts rewards for your instrument.
- Cantor8 pays you and each partner venue its share, on the agreed schedule.

## What you do

| Step                                  | Who                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Hold a featured-app right             | Your featured-app party |
| Tell Cantor8 the share each venue gets | You                    |
| Create the delegation, one time       | Your featured-app party |

The featured-app party holds the network featured-app right. That right is a network contract. It is separate from the suite DAR.

### Create the delegation

The delegation lets Cantor8 post rewards for this instrument. Your featured-app party submits it once.

<Steps>
<Step title="Send the BatchedMarkersProxy details">

Send Cantor8 the `BatchedMarkersProxy` of your featured-app party: `templateId`, `contractId`, and `createdEventBlob`. Read them from the participant that hosts that party.

</Step>

<Step title="Upload the FaMarkerDelegation DAR">

Cantor8 sends you the `FaMarkerDelegation` DAR. Upload and vet it on the participant that hosts your featured-app party.

</Step>

<Step title="Submit the create command">

Cantor8 sends a create command. Fill in `userId` and `batchedMarkersProxyCid`. Submit it as your featured-app party. That creates the delegation and lets Cantor8 post rewards for this instrument.

</Step>
</Steps>

Token App does not take part in this step. Its ledger user needs `CanActAs` for the admin and the issuer only.

The featured-app party can revoke the delegation. Cantor8 then stops posting rewards for this instrument.

## After launch

| Event                          | What you do                                         |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| A new partner venue            | Tell Cantor8 the share that venue gets              |
| A venue share changes          | Tell Cantor8 the new share                          |
| A venue transfers the instrument | Send that venue the details in [Other venues](/token-factory/venue-partners) |
